How far we travel from Tavares

Dispatch is based in Tavares, so the closest towns are the easiest to reach. Eustis is about 5 miles from Tavares, Mount Dora about 6 and Leesburg about 10. Umatilla sits roughly 14 miles north on State Road 19.

We do not promise arrival times, because traffic, the weather and the night's other calls change them. The dispatcher tells you what is realistic when you call.

Town by town: ZIP codes and what to know

Eustis uses ZIP 32726 for the city and 32736 for an area outside the city limits that the county administers. Callers in that second area should tell the dispatcher so the right utility and building office are named on the job.

Mount Dora is ZIP 32757, with 32756 used for PO boxes. Its downtown is a historic district, so the plumber asks the age of the building before choosing materials.

Leesburg covers ZIPs 34748 and 34788 and runs a large city water system that serves addresses inside and outside the city limits. Umatilla is ZIP 32784, and its water report describes treatment for a sulfur smell.

Each of those facts is explained on the town page, along with a short checklist for the minutes before a plumber arrives. Read the page for your town while you wait for a call back, or before you need us.

How dispatch works across town lines

The dispatcher asks for your street address first, because a house a few blocks over the line can sit in a different city or in the unincorporated county. That detail decides which water utility, sewer system and permit office applies to your job.

If you are not sure which side of a boundary you are on, say so. The plumber will check the address records on the way, and if the City handles part of the problem, we tell you which department to call.

Heavy rain and flooding can stretch the day for everyone around the lakes. When the line is busy with several urgent calls, the dispatcher tells you honestly where you stand in the order and what to shut off while you wait.

Tavares itself

Tavares has one ZIP code, 32778. It sits on the isthmus between Lake Eustis and Lake Dora, with US 441 and State Road 19 running through town. Our address is 1807 Salk Dr, near the hospital and the Lakeview office area.

Around Tavares the usual calls involve slab leaks, older downtown pipes and lots that stay wet near the lakes. Browse our full list of plumbing services to see which fits your problem.

What changes from town to town

Each town has its own water system, its own building department and its own housing stock. Eustis and Mount Dora have historic downtowns with older pipes, while Leesburg has a large municipal system and Umatilla sits near the Ocala National Forest.

We write those differences into each town page so you know what to ask your utility or building department. Permit rules vary by city and follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, the state standard, so the plumber will tell you whom to contact for your address.

If you are unsure whether your street falls inside a city or in the unincorporated county, ask the dispatcher. The answer decides which utility and which building office applies to your job.
