Contact Tavares 24/7 Plumbing

The fastest way to reach Tavares 24/7 Plumbing is the phone number on this page, and a dispatcher answers at any hour. We do not use contact forms, because plumbing problems need a conversation.

Below is everything else you may need, from our address to the details that help a dispatcher the most.

Call (855) 575-2890 now.

How to reach us

Call the number on this page for emergencies and for planned work, such as a water heater swap or a fixture install. The line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays.

If you prefer email for a non-urgent question, write to contact@247plumbertavares.com. Email is not monitored like the phone line, so call for anything involving water, sewage or a dead water heater.

What to tell the dispatcher

A few details let the dispatcher choose the right plumber and parts. Short answers are fine.

  • Your street address and nearest cross street or landmark.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and where in the house.
  • Whether you have closed the main water valve.
  • The rough age of the house and whether it sits on a slab.
  • Any gate codes or pets the plumber should know about.

If you are not sure about any answer, say so. The plumber will check on arrival and explain what was found.

Business details

Tavares 24/7 Plumbing is based at 1807 Salk Dr, Tavares, FL 32778, in Lake County near the Lakeview office area and the hospital. Work is dispatched by phone, so please call before you visit.

Hours: open 24 hours a day, every day. Service area: Eustis, Mount Dora, Leesburg, Umatilla and the rest of the Tavares area.

Before you call

If water is running, close the main valve first, then call. If you smell gas or see smoke, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility before you call us.

A dispatcher can only help with plumbing, so for fire, medical trouble or electrical danger, call 911 instead. Plumbing work here follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, the standard behind local permits and inspections.
