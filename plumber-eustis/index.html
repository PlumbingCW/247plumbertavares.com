Before We Arrive in Eustis

These four steps limit damage in the first few minutes. None of them needs tools.

Find the main shutoff.

Look near the hose bib at the front of the house or in the meter box. Older Eustis homes may have a stiff gate valve, so turn it slowly and tell the dispatcher if it will not move.

Stop using every drain.

If a toilet or tub is backing up, hold off on flushing, laundry and the dishwasher. Every gallon you send down goes to the same blocked spot.

Switch off power near water.

Flip the breakers for wet rooms and stay out of standing water if you are unsure. Water in an outlet is a bigger emergency than the leak.

Photograph before cleanup.

Take clear photos of the damage and the failed part before anything is mopped or moved. Your insurance carrier will ask for them.

What an 1883 downtown does to plumbing

Eustis was incorporated in 1883, and the Eustis Commercial Historic District, which includes City Hall and the Iron Block Building, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Victorian homes and early twentieth-century buildings stand around it. A house that old has usually been re-plumbed in pieces, so one wall can hold copper, galvanized steel and plastic joined by transition fittings.

Those mixed joints are where leaks begin. We trace and isolate the failing section first, then cut, so plaster and trim are disturbed as little as possible.

Cast iron and clay under old floors

Drains from the early and mid twentieth century are often cast iron or clay. Cast iron rusts from the inside and clay cracks at joints where roots enter. A repeat backup in an older house is a reason to run a camera, not to keep snaking the same line.

Newer streets and the 32736 area

Away from downtown, newer homes use PVC drains and PEX or CPVC supply lines, which fail in different ways. Homes in 32736 sit outside city limits, so say when you call if you are on city sewer or septic. It changes where we look first.

The water that reaches Eustis taps

The City of Eustis draws Floridan Aquifer groundwater from nine wells. Its 2023 water quality report gives a chlorine residual averaging 1.2 ppm and a copper 90th percentile of 0.11 ppm. Small separate systems also exist, such as the Heathrow Country Estates plant, so ask the utility which one serves your address.

The report lists no hardness figure. If you see crusty fixtures or a water heater that rumbles, test your tap with a home kit before assuming scale is the cause, because sediment and a failing element look similar.

A note on freezes and outdoor lines

The Great Freeze of 1894-95 and another in 1898-99 wrecked the local citrus economy. Hard freezes are rare now, but a cold night can still crack an exposed hose bib or irrigation stub. Cover them on a freeze forecast, and call if one splits.
