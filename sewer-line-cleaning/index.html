Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Tavares, FL

.

Sewer line cleaning in Tavares, FL clears the main drain that carries everything from your house out to the street or septic tank. When sewage backs up into a tub or floor drain, call our 24/7 line, stop using water and keep people and pets away until help arrives.

A backup in the lowest drain of the house usually means the main line is blocked, not a single sink. A licensed plumber clears it with a cable or a hydro jet, tells you what was found, and explains the price before work starts. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

Backup safety steps: what to do right now

Sewage carries bacteria and parasites, so safety comes before cleanup. Run through this list while you wait.

  • Stop all water use: no flushing, laundry or dishwasher.
  • Keep children, pets and anyone with health problems out of the area.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a main line backup.
  • Wear rubber gloves and boots if you must enter the wet area.
  • Switch off power to any outlet or appliance sitting in standing water.
  • Photograph the damage before anything is moved.

If neighbors have the same problem, the blockage may be in the City main. The City of Tavares lists an emergency sewer line on its Wastewater Division page, and a call to them at the same time is sensible.

What main line cleaning involves

A main line is the larger pipe, usually four inches across, that collects every drain in the house. It runs to the cleanout, a capped pipe in the yard, and then on to the street. We open the cleanout and feed in a powered cable with a cutting head, which bores through a clog and cuts roots at the walls.

When grease or heavy scale lines the pipe, a cable only makes a hole through it. A hydro jet follows with high-pressure water that scours the wall clean. Our hydro-jetting service explains that method, and it is often the better answer for a line that clogs again and again.

Sewage in the shower? Do not run any more water, call the line that never closes.

Where your cleanout is and why it matters

A cleanout is a capped pipe, often white or black plastic, in the yard near the house or by the foundation. Some homes have two, one at the house and one near the property line. If we can open one, the cable goes straight into the main without pulling a toilet.

If you cannot find one

Look along the side of the house where the bathrooms are, and near any outdoor faucet. Under heavy plants and old mulch, a cap can be hidden. If there is none, we can use a toilet or a roof vent, and adding a cleanout is a good fix for next time.

Storm drains and yard drains

Yard drains, downspout lines and storm drains run separately from the sanitary sewer. They fill with leaves, sand and roots, and they overflow in heavy rain. Cleaning them with a cable or jet restores the flow, and it keeps stormwater away from the foundation.

Never connect a storm drain to the sewer line. If you notice water from a yard drain running into a cleanout or toilet during storms, tell us, since a cross-connection or a damaged joint may be sending rainwater into the sewer.

Heavy rain, flooding and the sewer line

Central Florida can see record rain. In November 2025, Mount Dora lifted a precautionary boil water notice after record rainfall and localized flooding, a reminder that storms strain drains and sewers across Lake County. Water-logged soil and overloaded mains are a common reason a line backs up during or just after a big storm.

If your backup begins during heavy rain and neighbors report the same, the City main is the likely cause. If it is only your house, the problem is on your line. We serve Mount Dora, Eustis and Tavares, and we tell you which side the clog is on.

What affects the cost of cleaning a main line

A main line cleaning is priced by the situation, and we explain it before work starts. These factors matter most.

Cleanout access.

A cleanout in the yard makes the job quick. Pulling a toilet or using a roof vent adds labor.

Cable or jet.

A cable clears most clogs. A hydro jet is a larger machine and a longer job.

Length of the run.

Longer runs to the street or a distant tank take more time and equipment.

Severity of the clog.

A soft clog is quick, while a root mass or packed grease needs several passes.

Camera follow-up.

If the line keeps clogging, a camera shows why, and that visit is priced separately.

Cleaning a clog is a maintenance job and is not usually covered. Damage from a sudden backup inside the home may be, so call your carrier, keep photos and save the plumber's invoice.

If the backup keeps coming back

A line that clogs again within weeks is telling you something. Roots, a sag in the pipe or a cracked joint can all trap waste. A sewer camera inspection shows which one it is, and if the pipe is damaged, repair is the lasting fix. Regular cleaning of a damaged line buys time, but does not solve it.
