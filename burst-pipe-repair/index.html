When a burst pipe is an emergency

Call right away if water is spraying, if a ceiling is sagging, or if the meter keeps spinning after every fixture is off. A slow drip under a sink can wait for a daytime visit. A split line behind drywall cannot.

Most Tavares homes sit on a concrete slab, so a break often shows up as a warm patch on the floor, a hissing sound in the wall, or water that appears at the garage door. Those signs point to a pressurized line failing, and every hour of delay makes the repair bigger.

What to do before we arrive

Four quick steps limit the damage. None of them needs tools, and all of them can be done in a few minutes.

Shut the main valve.

The main shutoff is usually at the front of the house near the hose bib or at the meter box. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Kill power to wet areas.

If water is near outlets, appliances or a panel, switch off those breakers. Stay out of standing water if you cannot be sure the power is off.

Drain the line.

Open the lowest faucet or hose bib. This releases pressure so less water reaches the break.

Move what you can.

Lift rugs, electronics and paper off the floor. Take photos of the damage for your insurance claim before anything is dried or removed.

If the water is coming from the street side of the meter, the pipe may belong to the city. The City of Tavares lists emergency water and sewer lines on its Water Division and Wastewater Division pages, and it maintains about 134 miles of water lines. Call us as well if you are not sure which side the break is on.

Why pipes burst in this part of Lake County

Freezes are rare in central Florida, so most bursts here have other causes. Age is the big one. Homes built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s often have polybutylene supply lines, which can crack at fittings without warning. Older homes may have galvanized steel that rusts from the inside until a section gives way.

Copper is not immune. Pinhole leaks and failed solder joints turn up in copper lines of every age, and a stuck pressure regulator can push line pressure high enough to split a fitting. A hard freeze does happen now and then, and a short cold snap can still crack an unprotected hose bib or an exposed line at the side of a house.

Lakefront and low lots

Tavares sits on a narrow strip between Lake Eustis and Lake Dora at roughly 118 feet above sea level. Lots near the water can have wet soil for long stretches, which speeds up corrosion on buried lines and keeps a slow leak hidden for weeks. If your yard stays soggy when it has not rained, ask for a leak check along with the repair.

How we repair a burst or broken line

The plumber confirms the shutoff holds, finds the break, and cuts out the damaged section. A clean repair means new pipe and new fittings, not a clamp over a weak spot. The job ends with the system pressurized and checked at every joint.

When one split reveals the pipe is near the end of its life, we say so and show the other choices. Replacing a single section fixes today's problem. Replacing the run, or a repiping project for the whole house, ends the pattern of new leaks every year or two.

What a burst pipe repair costs

The price depends on what the break looks like once the area is opened up. Five things move it most.

Where the pipe is.

A line in an open garage is quick. A line behind tile or under a slab takes cutting and patching.

Pipe material.

Copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized each need different fittings and labor.

How much pipe is bad.

One clean split is a small job. A run full of pinholes may justify replacing the whole run.

Water damage cleanup.

Plumbing stops the water. Drying and rebuilding is a separate job that often goes through insurance.

Time of day.

Nights and weekends can change the dispatch cost, and we tell you that before work starts.

Homeowners insurance often covers the damage a sudden burst causes, but may not pay to replace the failed pipe itself. Ask your carrier before you sign anything, and keep your photos.
