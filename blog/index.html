What the guides cover

The topics follow the questions people ask during real plumbing trouble. They include what a water heater swap involves, how leak sensors and meter tests work, why a toilet tank keeps leaking, and what a sewer camera can and cannot show.

There are also guides on drains, shower pans, garbage disposals, frost protection for pipes and leaks under a concrete slab. Each one is written for a Tavares home rather than for a generic house in another climate.

New guides are added as we see which questions come up again and again on the phone. If you want a topic covered, mention it to the dispatcher on your next call.

When a guide says call a plumber

Some jobs look simple and are not. Anything involving gas, a main sewer line, a pipe under a slab or a water heater that is leaking from the tank belongs to a licensed plumber, and the guides say so plainly.

A good rule is to stop if you cannot shut the water off, if the repair needs a permit or if a mistake would flood the house. Phone the dispatch line and describe where you stopped. That saves the plumber time and often saves you money.
